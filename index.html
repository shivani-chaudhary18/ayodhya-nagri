Questions

FAQ for travelers & vendors

How do payments work?

For souvenirs you can pay cash on delivery or via online link. Hotel blocks are held without prepayment; final payment goes directly to the property on check-in unless you request a prepaid link.

Can I get airport/rail pickup?

Yes. Share your flight/train number in the plan form notes; we arrange a driver who knows temple-side drop points and luggage rules.

How are vendors verified?

We call each shop, verify address/ID, and place a test order. New listings stay in “pending” until that check is done.

What if I only need darshan help?

Select “Guided circuit” in the plan form. We’ll skip hotels and souvenirs and just share the timetable and local help.
